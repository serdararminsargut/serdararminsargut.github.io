Agency Review

What can a brand confirm in the first 30 seconds?

1. Clear casting details

Birth year, current height, eye and hair colour, location, skills and parent-managed contact are presented clearly. A full birth date, school and personal phone number are not published.

2. Curated photographs

A natural headshot, smiling portrait, full-length image, formal look, sportswear and lifestyle frames are selected without repetition. Relevance and quality matter more than image count.

3. Fast showreel access

Face, voice, natural expression and on-camera confidence appear immediately. Video players load only after a visitor chooses to play, protecting mobile performance.

4. International context

The English presentation explains suitability for commercials, catalog, fashion, sportswear, e-commerce and brand work while accurately stating that the talent is based in Istanbul.

Professional Fit

Strong information without inflated claims.

  • The Turkish, English and Arabic agency pages use reciprocal hreflang links for the same professional purpose.
  • No false location claim such as “Dubai-based model” is used. The accurate wording is “Istanbul-based; international projects can be reviewed.”
  • Sportswear, footwear, formal, catalog and e-commerce work are represented through distinct visual selections.
  • Brand name, usage countries and platforms, usage term, shoot date, travel requirements, fee and contract party must be provided in writing.
  • Direct contact with the child, requests for a personal phone number, school details or live location are not accepted.
